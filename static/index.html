Whole-of-Australian-Government CBRN Response Architecture

Institutional, statutory, and computational design overview of PandemicPrepDash.

Statutory Frameworks

  • National Health Security Act 2007: Regulates Security Sensitive Biological Agents (SSBA Tier 1 and 2), mandatory notification to ACDC.
  • Biosecurity Act 2015: Empowers DAFF and ACVO for border quarantine and emergency animal disease response.
  • Therapeutic Goods Act 1989: Section 19A emergency exemptions and rapid provisional authorization by the TGA.
  • Gene Technology Act 2000: OGTR oversight of viral vectors, modified organisms, and synthetic biology constructs.

Agentic Team Topology

Each workflow node is assigned a specialized multi-agent squad. Squads run through observation, hypothesis, tool execution, and synthesis cycles, recording structured data to a shared blackboard.

• Bioinformatics Squad: BLAST, clade typing, mutation calling
• Structural Biology: AlphaFold/ESMFold inference, binding pocket volume
• Medicinal Chemistry: In silico docking, ARTG register cross-check
• Vaccine Squad: Neutralizing epitope prediction, mRNA formulations
• Biosecurity Squad: SSBA classifications & dual-use compliance
• Policy Liaison: Whole-of-Government situational report synthesis

Adaptive DAG Execution Model

The pipeline models the response pathway as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with dependency tracking via NetworkX. When an upstream node finishes, its outputs populate the shared blackboard and downstream nodes are unblocked. Nodes requiring Human-in-the-Loop review (e.g. CBRN Biosecurity classification) automatically pause until confirmed by an authorized operator.
