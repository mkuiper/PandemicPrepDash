Whole-of-Australian-Government CBRN Response Architecture

Institutional, statutory, and computational design overview developed under Australian AISI and Home Affairs guidance.

Statutory Emergency Frameworks

  • National Health Security Act 2007: Regulates Security Sensitive Biological Agents (SSBA Tier 1 and 2), mandatory notification to ACDC within 24h.
  • Biosecurity Act 2015: Empowers DAFF and ACVO for border quarantine and emergency animal disease response.
  • Therapeutic Goods Act 1989: Section 19A emergency exemptions and rapid provisional authorization by the TGA.
  • Australian Radiation Protection and Nuclear Safety Act 1998: ARPANSA statutory Emergency Reference Levels and public dose thresholds.
  • Gene Technology Act 2000: OGTR oversight of viral vectors, modified organisms, and synthetic biology constructs.

Agentic Team Topology & Safety

Agents use strict non-anthropomorphic identifiers (`AGENT-BIOINFO-LEAD-01`, etc.) to adhere to Australian AISI AI safety guidelines. Each node has a Node Lead who coordinates with other node leads via auditable message buses.

• Genomics Lead Agent: BLAST, clade typing, mutation calling
• Structural Biology Lead: AlphaFold/ESMFold inference, binding pocket volume
• Medicinal Chemistry Lead: In silico docking, ARTG register cross-check
• Vaccinology Lead: Neutralizing epitope prediction, mRNA formulations
• Health Physics Lead: Gamma spectrometry, HYSPLIT plume dispersion
• Biosecurity Intelligence Lead: SSBA classifications & dual-use compliance
• Policy Liaison Lead: Whole-of-Government situational report synthesis

PandemicPrepDash User & Operator Guide

Co-developed by Australian AISI & Department of Home Affairs.

AISI AI Safety & Non-Anthropomorphic Agent Design

In strict compliance with Australian AI Safety Institute (AISI) governance principles, all agents in this platform use transparent, functional designations (e.g. AGENT-BIOINFO-LEAD-01) rather than human names, ensuring operators never mistake synthetic agent reasoning for human authority.

Inter-Node Lead Orchestration & Auditable Message Bus

Every node in the DAG is led by an autonomous Node Lead agent. When executing, node leads coordinate with upstream and peer node leads to request specific coordinates, biological conformations, or dose models. All inter-node dialogues are logged with timestamps and content for retrospective auditing.

Radiological CBRN Emergency Responses

The platform supports full radiological emergency scenarios (such as an orphaned Caesium-137 source or RDD dirty bomb). The workflow models HPGe gamma photopeaks (661.7 keV), HYSPLIT atmospheric plume deposition, Prussian Blue decorporation antidotes, and ARPANSA/ANSTO statutory briefings.

Local Open-Weight Models & Sovereign Cloud Support

Each node can be configured to execute using local open-weight models (via vLLM or Ollama, running Llama-3.3-70B, DeepSeek-R1, or Qwen-2.5), sovereign IRAP-protected government enclaves, or Google Vertex AI in Sydney.
